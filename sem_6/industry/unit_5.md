## AR Technology

- overlays digital information onto the real world environment
- combines the physical world and computer generated content
- enhances interaction with the physical environment in real time

### Types of AR Technology

- Marker Based AR
  - uses markers such as QR code/images
  - display digital content when marker is recognized
- Markless AR
  - uses GPS, compass, location information
- Projection Based AR
  - projects digital images or light onto physical surfaces
  - users interact with the projected content
- Overlay Based AR
  - replaces or enhances the real view with digital objects

## AR Hardware

- physical devices
- capture real world → process info → display virtual object

### Input Devices

- interaction between user and AR system
- accept user commands

### Sensors

- used to track the position and movement of users and objects
- merge real and virtual env

### Display Devices

- Display digital information over the real world
- head mounted display, smart classes, etc
